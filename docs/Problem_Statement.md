# SmartProcure — Problem Statement & Business Domain

## 1. Problem Statement
Organizations frequently encounter bottlenecks, lack of transparency, and unauthorized expenditure when processing employee purchase requests through informal email threads, spreadsheets, or paper forms.

### Core Operational Inefficiencies:
- **Unclear Responsibility:** Lack of defined approval routing leads to delayed purchases.
- **No Real-Time Budget Controls:** Requests get approved without automated department budget checks.
- **Auditability Deficits:** Inability to produce verifiable audit records of who authorized a purchase and why.
- **Lack of Enforcement:** Employees or managers bypassing business rules or self-approving requests.

## 2. Proposed Solution
SmartProcure is a multi-tier, role-based Purchase Order Approval Workflow System built using **Spring Boot 3**, **React**, and **MySQL**. It enforces structured state transitions, JWT-based security, department-level authorization, and complete workflow traceability.
